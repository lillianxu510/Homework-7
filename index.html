About Me

Hello! My name is Lillian Xu, and I am a student at Northeastern University. I am currently in my second year of majoring in Data Science! This website is to introduce myself, share my interests, and show some of my projects in my academic lifetime.

The reason I chose Data Science can be attributed to the courses I took in high school. I took several programming classes, where I started learning Python, Javascript, and Java. I enjoyed programming a lot, and knew I could do a lot with it. I also took a statistics class, in which I enjoyed finding patterns and trends. By combining my skills in programming, statistics, and mathematics, I realized Data Science was the perfect fit for me.

Throughout my college coursework, I have furthered my skills in Python, the very first language I have worked with. I used its tools and libraries like Pandas, NumPy, Altair, and other data visualization technologies. I have thoroughly enjoyed learning about different types of relationships and visualizations for supporting data, and these tools have allowed me to explore different aspects of Data Science.
